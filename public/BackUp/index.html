THE HUMAN BEHIND THE VOICE

HI, I'M
JACKOBY.

Artist at heart. Storyteller by nature.

I'm Jackoby Clinton, an American-Icelandic voice actor and professional singer. I graduated from the University of North Texas, and I've spent more than twenty years developing my life in music and performance.

My grandmother immigrated from Iceland and passed down our family's tradition of professional funeral singing, known in the tradition as wailing. When I was young, she made sure I received the highest level of training to become an Icelandic skáld—a poet and artist whose craft is treated with deep devotion.

That history is part of how I understand performance. I see the voice not just as sound, but as a physical, emotional instrument—and a way to connect with another person.

EXPLORE MY APPROACH ↘
SINGING · TEACHING · CONNECTION

TECHNIQUE
WITH HEART.

I've taught singing professionally for five years and have worked with more than thirty students. My approach blends modern vocal technique with the expressive ideas I have learned through performance and Icelandic tradition.

My signature lesson, the Three Pillars of Singing, explores how the human voice uses air. In lessons and workshops, I invite singers to understand the body as an instrument, connect technique with emotion, and make performance feel personal.
